The story behind DevelopedByK

Meet the founder

Dr. Kenya Andrews

Weddings are in our blood! Born and raised in Macon, GA, our CEO, Founder, and Owner, Dr. Kenya Andrews, studied wedding execution from her father, who is a wedding photographer, several bridal consultants, and numerous wedding planners. To date, she has assisted with nearly 200 weddings! And yes, she does have a PhD in Computer Science, highly unrelated!

Dr. Andrews prides herself on being fully transparent, honest, and surrendered to Jesus Christ. She has been immersed in wedding and event planning for years and hopes to continue her journey by curating your special day, start to finish.

Philosophy

We plan the way you'd want a bridge built — with intent behind every measurement.

Behind every beautiful wedding day is a hundred small decisions made correctly and in the right order. That's the part most people don't see, and it's the part we specialize in.

We start with your hopes for the day, translate them into a real plan — vendors, timeline, budget, logistics — and then coordinate every moving piece so the day itself feels effortless, because the effort already happened.

Tailored to you. Structured underneath. That combination is the whole craft.
