WEDDING PLANNER & COORDINATOR

Dr. Kenya Andrews, PhD

Weddings are in our blood! Born and raised in Macon, GA, our CEO, Founder, and Owner, Dr. Kenya Andrews, studied wedding execution from her father, who is a wedding photographer, several bridal consultants, and numerous wedding planners.

To date, she has assisted with nearly 200 weddings! And yes, she does have a PhD in Computer Science, highly unrelated!

Dr. Andrews prides herself on being fully transparent, honest, and prepared. She seeks to honor Jesus Christ in all she does, walking faithfully in His love and calling.

She has been immersed in wedding and event planning for years and hopes to continue her journey by curating your special day, start to finish.
